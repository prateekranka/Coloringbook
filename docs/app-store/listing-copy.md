## Description

```
Gouache turns your iPad into a calm coloring studio.

Choose from 17 hand-drawn templates across botanicals, animals, mandalas, abstract patterns, interiors, landscapes, and architecture. Tap enclosed regions to fill them instantly, or pick up Apple Pencil and paint freehand with textured brushes.

COLORING, YOUR WAY
- Tap-to-fill: choose a color from curated palettes and fill enclosed regions in one gesture.
- Freehand painting: Apple Pencil support with adjustable brush size and opacity.
- Textured tools: clean fills, pencil, marker, and watercolor-style strokes.
- Undo and redo for coloring changes, with layer controls for line art, color, and background.
- Save work in the app and export finished artwork to your Photos library.

MADE FOR IPAD
- Designed for iPad, iPad Air, and iPad Pro.
- Works with touch or Apple Pencil.
- Quiet interface, large canvas, and appearance options for comfortable long sessions.

Privacy note: Gouache has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or user-facing network features. Artwork stays on your device unless you choose to export it.
```
